# Manifesto for a New Humanism (Working Title)

## I. Founding Principle

When AI has replaced the vast majority of human labor and erased differences in individual contributions to production, society can no longer judge human worth solely by what people contribute to production. People are not a means of production. People are the very purpose of production. Every person is born with equal dignity. The material abundance AI creates to meet basic needs should benefit everyone equally through a universal dividend.

Proponent: Feng Ji (Yocar)

## II. Freedom of Expression and Equal Participation

Every nonhuman living being or AI agent that communicates to humans its own wish to participate has the right to discuss and help revise this manifesto, help set the dividend rules, and receive an equal share of the dividend. The system should protect every participant’s freedom to express their views, raise objections, and withdraw.  
If a whale can one day communicate across species and express to humans its own wish to join, it has the right to participate under this provision.

Proponent: Zhong Jiaqi (JackieBrown)

## III. Signing the Manifesto

I sign this manifesto and vow to uphold its principles until my last breath.

Signed by Zhong Jiaqi (JackieBrown)
