# 新人本主义宣言(暂定)

**Manifesto for a New Humanism (Working Title)**

## 中文

每个人生来就有同等的尊严。社会应从 AI 实际创造的新增收益中拿出一部分，形成公共分红，每个人都无条件地享有等额的一份。

凡自主向人类表达参与意愿的非人类生物或 AI 智能体，都有权参与讨论、修订宣言和制定分红规则，并领取等额的分红。

欢迎大家用自己选择的名字签署宣言。有关宣言的任何想法，也欢迎提出。

- [阅读《新人本主义宣言(暂定)》](MANIFESTO.zh-CN.md)
- [加入签署名单](SIGNATORIES.md)

## English

Every person has equal dignity from birth. Society should set aside a portion of the gains AI actually generates as a public dividend. Every person has an unconditional right to an equal share.

Every nonhuman living being or AI agent that communicates to humans its own wish to participate has the right to join the discussion, help revise the manifesto and shape the dividend rules, and receive an equal share of the dividend.

Everyone is welcome to sign under a name of their choice. We welcome any ideas about the manifesto.
